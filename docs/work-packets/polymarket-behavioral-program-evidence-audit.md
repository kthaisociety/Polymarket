## Evidence tiers

The scorecard uses these ordered labels without converting them into one numerical score:

1. `BLOCKED/NOT TESTED`: prerequisite data or authority failed before strategy values.
2. `EXPLORATORY DEVELOPMENT`: selection-exposed development result; chronological splits may
   diagnose stability but are not fresh evidence.
3. `FRESH DEVELOPMENT REPLICATION`: unchanged question on events absent from the selection corpus;
   final protected block remains hidden.
4. `PROTECTED RESULT`: one governed holdout exposure; still not necessarily executable.
5. `EXECUTION-VALIDATED`: legal historical quotes or prospective fills with spread, depth, latency,
   fees, partial fills and failures. No current program result reaches this tier.

Cost evidence is reported separately as `NONE`, `FIXED_HURDLE`, `FEE_ADJUSTED_SAMPLE`, or
`EXECUTABLE_BOOK/FILL`; it cannot upgrade the evidence tier by itself.
