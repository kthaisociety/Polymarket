# Work packet: Polymarket external-signal candidate scan

## Identity

- Packet: `polymarket.scan-development-external-signal-candidates`.
- Owning responsibility: development-only constrained exploration and conditional public-source
  expansion.
- Input repository state: `historical checkpoint 43bb5c5c` on

## Objective

Inspect only the authorized development-event metadata in the retained Polymarket corpus and
identify a small number of contracts for which a legally timed public series could support either
a cost-aware tradable hypothesis or a reproducible predictive mechanism. Do not acquire a source
until one exact field, publication clock, join, baseline, and falsifier are named.

  first 24 event groups/155 markets are development, final 7 groups/45 markets stay protected.
- Permitted scan fields: development event/market identity, question/outcome label, lifecycle

- Explicit exclusions: production writes, protected metadata or values, current web values treated

  production data service, and sibling repositories.
- Existing XTracker evidence is prior negative ancestry: a high-dimensional count/pace ridge failed

  establish a general model.

## Fixed opening analysis

Source 01 returns the exact development event/question inventory and coverage only. Candidate
selection then follows these priorities:

1. public source semantics match the contract settlement variable directly;
2. source observations have a defensible historical `available_at` clock;
3. the corpus contains repeated decisions or multiple comparable events;
4. the mechanism determines a signed price implication or movement-timing implication;
5. a no-change/current-price baseline and an execution-delay/cost falsifier are possible.

At most three candidates may be retained from the inventory. Only one may receive a source-scoping
follow-up before the scan packet closes. Selection is semantic and coverage-based, not based on
looking at that candidate's future prices or payout.
