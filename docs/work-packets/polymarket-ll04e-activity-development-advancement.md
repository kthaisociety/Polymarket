## Candidate memo

### Exact question and ancestry

For the unchanged LL-03 or LL-05 taker parent, does
`ll04d-latency-scaled-primary-v1` improve net displayed-touch PnL per one-share collateral-second
over complete activity history and both one-component gates with adequate coverage, chronological
sign stability, and no dominant positive block?

The parent attempts retain their original decisions, marketable-touch entries/exits, costs,
holding periods, missing outcomes, and label maturity. LL-04e evaluates the immutable LL-04c
campaign and performs no fitting. Its only permitted positive disposition is a new prospective
paper-execution study.

### Fixed development standard

`ll04e-primary-development-v1` requires:

- at least 10 chronological collector blocks;
- at least 80% of blocks scored for the primary contrast;
- at least 30 scorable joint-gate attempts;
- joint scorable retention of at least 25% versus complete history;
- unresolved entered outcomes no greater than 10% of joint entries;
- strictly positive primary improvement in at least 60% of scored blocks;
- largest positive block contribution no greater than 50% of total positive block improvement;
- strictly positive pooled, block-macro, early-half, and late-half primary values;
- strictly positive pooled joint-minus-quiet-only and joint-minus-recent-activity-only primary
  metric values.

The required campaign primary is `JOINT_MINUS_COMPLETE_HISTORY` using
`NET_TOUCH_PNL_PER_COLLATERAL_SECOND_CHANGE`. All fractions keep missing denominators explicit.

### Dispositions and stopping rule

- Any coverage-clause failure returns `INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE`; it does not convert missing evidence
  into rejection or success.
- With sufficient coverage, any economic/stability/component clause failure returns
  `REJECT_DEVELOPMENT`.
- Only all-clause passage returns `ADVANCE_TO_PAPER_EXECUTION_STUDY`.
- No alternate threshold may be tried under this candidate after campaign values are inspected.
  Any meaning-changing revision needs a new policy/standard identity and retained ancestry.

Recommendation before data: `EVALUATE`; no empirical disposition exists.
