# Backtest-gate principles

## Protected evidence has a different meaning

- Development cross-validation supports iteration; protected evidence tests a frozen candidate.
- Freeze target, feature set, model, fitted procedures, training protocol, evaluation plan, and
  claim before protected content is read.
- Record exposure before or at disclosure. Failure after disclosure does not restore freshness.
- The researcher may request evaluation but cannot issue its own permit or promotion.

## Cross-validation must reproduce legal decisions

- At each decision time, rebuild predictor information and matured training labels independently.
- Fit every learned operation inside the declared training window and keep prediction inference-only.
- Purge when label-information intervals overlap. Add an embargo only for an additional named
  dependence threat; do not use either as ceremony.
- Preserve failed fits, unavailable labels, insufficient windows, and missing predictions in the
  evidence and coverage denominator.

## Selection changes the burden of proof

- Count and preserve all tried targets, features, transformations, models, thresholds, metrics,
  windows, and rejected candidates through ancestry.
- Challenge the gap between the best trial and simple or persistence baselines.
- Inspect performance across time, entities, regimes, seeds, and defensible protocol choices.
- Treat concentrated gains, unstable signs, narrow coverage, and sensitivity to one date or entity
  as primary evidence, not footnotes.
- Use multiple-testing or selection-adjusted measures only when their assumptions match the task;
  ancestry remains mandatory even when no correction is appropriate.

## Predeclaration prevents result shopping

- Fix scenarios, decision schedule, data scope, refit policy, metrics, baselines, slices,
  robustness views, failure rules, claim, and promotion standard before exposure.
- Evaluate the exact frozen procedure. An undeclared fallback, skipped date, replacement model, or
  repaired feature is a failed execution.
- Keep historical, alternative-path, and synthetic evidence distinct. Synthetic success cannot
  overwrite historical failure.

## Measurement is not judgment

- The backtest engine records what happened; the evaluator measures; an independent validator
  applies the predeclared standard.
- One scalar cannot hide failure coverage, instability, concentration, or adverse subgroups.
- A promoted scientific finding remains conditional on its historical world and assumptions; it is
  not automatically a business decision or timeless causal truth.
- A rejection remains useful history and may motivate a new, explicitly descended candidate.

## Optional financial-model challenges

- Verify that any activity/event sampling rule, transformation order, overlap weight, resampling
  scheme, ensemble composition, feature diagnostic, and hyperparameter search was fixed or learned
  inside its declared boundary.
- Purge exact overlapping label intervals. Add embargo only for a named residual dependence threat;
  neither repairs non-point-in-time data.
- Treat out-of-bag scores, in-sample importance, repeated development folds, and the selected metric
  as exposed evidence. Challenge correlated substitutes, unstable interactions, and hidden trials.
- Probability loss, calibration, classification, continuous error, and economic metrics answer
  different questions. Require the metric and claim to match without adding undeclared execution
  assumptions.
