# Start Polymarket Research

Begin from an exact authorized market scope, not from a favorite method.

## Establish the boundary

Read [references/polymarket-boundary.md](references/polymarket-boundary.md) completely. Resolve:

- explicit contract IDs and an aware research decision time;
- exact registered fields, dimensions, coverage, vintage, and source digest;
- the proved `human_ro` read-only session when using production data;
- unresolved event meaning, outcome mapping, availability, grouping, and snapshot semantics.

Stop instead of inventing any missing authority-critical fact.

## Open one research branch

1. State a narrow economic or microstructure question and at least one rival explanation.
2. Choose the smallest authorized contract population capable of distinguishing them. Do not call
   related tokens or contracts independent without authoritative event/group identity.
3. Declare the observation clock and sampling rule. Clock time, trade count, traded size, notional,
   flow imbalance, and event-triggered samples are different views, not interchangeable defaults.
4. Use `$analyze-time-series-data` to author the first falsifying analysis through the constrained
   surface. Preserve exact code, parameters, actual accesses, receipt, and result identity.
5. Retain a null or mechanically explained result. Only a surviving mechanism may continue to
   `$design-time-series-feature-set` and harness-owned temporal development.
6. Use `$review-backtest-gate` only after development evidence is credible and the real Polymarket
   protected-data prerequisites are satisfied.

## Select ideas without prescribing them

The larger [financial-ML idea catalog](../../../docs/research-guidance/financial-machine-learning-ideas.md)
contains optional examples grouped by the problem they may help diagnose. Do not load it by default.
Consult only a relevant section when the current question needs alternatives—for example sampling,
path-aware labels, overlap, transforms, ensembles, diagnostics, or tuning.

For every selected idea, record:

- the mechanism or failure mode that makes it relevant here;
- required fields and temporal/legal prerequisites;
- fixed versus learned choices and where learning occurs;
- a simpler comparison and a result that would falsify it;
- the trial-budget cost and ancestry created by trying it.

The catalog is a menu of possible probes. It is not evidence, a checklist to exhaust, or a default
pipeline.

## Return a startup memo

Return the exact scope and caveats, question and rivals, first analysis, optional idea families worth
considering, explicit exclusions, falsifier, next skill, and epistemic status `EXPLORATORY`.

Never claim a legal resolution label, immutable historical snapshot, independent cross-contract
replication, freeze eligibility, validation, promotion, or durable memory merely from this memo.
